## What the bot does

JaxBot can:

- Greet new members in a channel you choose

You choose which servers invite the bot. You can kick or remove it at any time.

## How the bot behaves

Commands only work in servers where the bot is present. Unknown commands are ignored. If the bot cannot send a message because of missing permissions, it will not retry or notify you in that channel.

Welcome messages are sent only if `WELCOME_CHANNEL_ID` is set and the bot can post in that channel.
